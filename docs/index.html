00 / Thesis

AI should do more than understand the world. It should help us design what comes next.

I build foundation models, generative systems, and the training infrastructure behind them, with a focus on accelerating science and engineering.

My work has moved across speech recognition, medical imaging, and atomic simulation: from Deep Speech 2 and billion-parameter multilingual models, to fastMRI, UMA, and generative models for matter.

01 / Research arc

One question, pursued across domains

What becomes possible when models, data, and compute scale together?

01 2015–2022

Scaling intelligence

From speech to billion-parameter systems

Co-created Deep Speech 2, then built and led FAIR Speech as the field moved from task-specific models toward large-scale, multilingual learning.

  • Human-level English and Mandarin speech recognition
  • Billion-parameter, 50+ language speech models
  • Production systems serving billions of users
02 2018–2023

Seeing faster

AI-accelerated MRI, from benchmark to clinic

Led fastMRI with NYU Langone, opening the datasets and models that helped establish the clinical standard for learned MRI reconstruction.

  • Up to 4× faster clinical scans
  • Prospective deployment at NYU Langone
  • Open benchmark adopted across the field
03 2020–2026

Modeling matter

Foundation models and open data for atoms

Helped build FAIR Chemistry and the open data, distributed training methods, and universal atomic models that changed the scale of computational chemistry.

  • ~500M unique structures used to train UMA
  • Graph Parallel training at billion-parameter scale
  • Hundreds of millions of open DFT calculations
04 2024–present

Designing the physical world

Generative AI that moves from prediction to invention

Developing flow-matching systems, high-throughput crystal discovery, and the foundations of an Artificial General Engineer at Prometheus.

  • FlowMM and FlowLLM for materials generation
  • FastCSP cuts crystal prediction from weeks to hours
  • Now building at Prometheus
